Nexus Tech Industries • San Francisco, CA (Hybrid)

About the Role

We are seeking a visionary Senior Product Designer to join our core team. You will be responsible for shaping the future of our flagship analytics platform, turning complex data into intuitive, engaging user experiences. You'll work closely with engineering, product management, and user research to deliver solutions that are both beautiful and highly functional.

In this role, you will champion a user-centered design process, from generative research and rapid prototyping to high-fidelity visual design and design system management. If you are passionate about elegant problem solving and have a portfolio demonstrating high-impact B2B software, we want to hear from you.

Key Responsibilities

  • check_circle Lead the end-to-end design process for major product features.
  • check_circle Establish and evolve the Nexus design system for consistency and scale.
  • check_circle Conduct user research and usability testing to validate design decisions.
  • check_circle Mentor junior designers and foster a culture of design excellence.

Requirements

Figma Master Prototyping User Research B2B SaaS Design Systems Agile Methodology
  • 5+ years of experience designing complex digital products.
  • A strong portfolio demonstrating a refined visual aesthetic and deep understanding of UX principles.
  • Excellent communication and presentation skills.
  • Ability to thrive in a fast-paced, collaborative environment.
